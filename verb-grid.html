What the grid shows at a glance

PatternWhere it holds
Past endings are one set: -α, -ες, -ε, -αμε, -ατε, -ανaorist and imperfect, all three groups — only the stem differs
Future = θα + a present-shaped ending-ω, -εις, -ει, -ουμε, -ετε, -ουν, on either stem
θα + dependent vs θα + presentthe only difference is aspect: once (θα γράψω) vs repeatedly (θα γράφω)
Never an έ- augment after θα or ναθα γράψω, not θα έγραψα — the augment belongs to the past alone
The 1st and 2nd plural lose the έ-γράψαμε, γράψατε — three syllables already carry the stress
The perfect conjugates only έχωthe second word is frozen: έχω / έχεις / έχει γράψει
Passive 1st plural is -όμαστε, past -όμαστανthe one ending you will reach for wrongly
Passive aorist shifts the stress in the pluralπλύθηκα but πλυθήκαμε ⚠
είμαι / έχω have no aorist and no dependentone past each (ήμουν, είχα), one future each (θα είμαι, θα έχω)
One set of past endings — -α, -ες, -ε, -αμε, -ατε, -αν — serves the aorist and the imperfect; only the stem changes.   θα and να never take the έ- augment.   The perfect conjugates only έχω; the second word is frozen.   Passive 1st plural is the ending to watch: -όμαστε now, -όμασταν in the past.
