# Greek B1 — what the level adds

What **KLIK στα ελληνικά Β1** teaches that the A2 sheets do not. Nothing here is
general-knowledge B1 grammar: every block is read off a page of that book, and every
sheet names the pages it came from, so you can check it against the original.

> **Scope.** The A2 area next door is the whole of A2. This area is **only the delta** —
> the grammar B1 introduces on top of it. Where B1 extends a topic A2 already covers,
> the sheet opens with what A2 had and what B1 adds, and does not repeat the A2 table.

## Where the grammar is in the book

KLIK B1 is 383 pages and the grammar is not collected anywhere — it sits in nine units,
**two slots per unit**, listed in two separate Γραμματική columns on the contents spread
(σσ. 18–19). All 382 scanned pages were swept three ways — grammatical-term density, the
rule-box opener *«Δες με προσοχή τους παρακάτω πίνακες»*, and the vertical ΓΡΑΜΜΑΤΙΚΗ
banner — and nothing turned up outside those eighteen slots.

## How the book marks its own tables

Worth knowing before you read any sheet here, because the colours carry meaning and
this page keeps them:

| | |
|---|---|
| **blue cell** | the stress **moves** in that form — των αντ**ρ**ών, των ανθρ**ώ**πων, των γυναικ**ώ**ν |
| **red cell** | an irregular or second plural — οι συγγεν**είς**, οι εξετ**άσεις**, τα β**άρη** |

The book's own check question on σ.35 is *«Πρόσεξε τα ουσιαστικά που έχουν μπλε και
κόκκινο χρώμα. Τι παρατηρείς ως προς τον τονισμό τους;»* — the colouring **is** the lesson.
