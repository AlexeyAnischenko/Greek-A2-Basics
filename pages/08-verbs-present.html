08 · Present Tense (Ενεστώτας)

Greek verbs are cited by their 1st person singular: γράφω = "I write". The present covers both English "I write" and "I am writing".

Three conjugation groups — how to tell them apart

Group1st singular ends inStressExample
A-ωon the stemγράφω
B1-άω / -ώon the endingαγαπάω / αγαπώ
B2-ώ (never -άω)on the endingμπορώ
