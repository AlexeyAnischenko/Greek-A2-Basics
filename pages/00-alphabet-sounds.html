Five ways to write "i": ι, η, υ, ει, οι — all identical in sound. Spelling must be memorised. Two ways to write "o": ο, ω. Two ways to write "e": ε, αι.

Stress (τόνος) — the only accent mark

RuleDetail
Every word of 2+ syllableshas exactly one written accent: μαμά, τραπέζι
One-syllable wordsno accent…
…exceptπού (where?), πώς (how?), τί/τι, ή (or), πιό/πιο to disambiguate
Stress positiononly on one of the last three syllables
Stress movesάνθρωπος → ανθρώπου; μάθημα → μαθήματα (see Nouns, p. 4)
