Greek A2 — Grammar Drill Sheets

Modern Greek (Νέα Ελληνικά) grammar for A1→A2, written as tables you can cover and recite. No prose explanation longer than a line. Every ending you need is in a cell.

Scope is A2 only. Anything that belongs to B1 has been left out on purpose, so if a rule is not here, you do not need it yet.

Mirror of the Preply canvas (21 pages). Page numbers below are canvas pages.

Tools

⌨ Greek keyboardType Greek with accents and copy it out — for filling in online homework

How to drill

  1. Cover the right column, read the left, say the answer out loud.
  2. Do one table per sitting. Repeat yesterday's table first (2 minutes).
  3. When a table is 100% cold-recall twice in a row, move it to the "monthly" pile.
  4. Verbs: always drill all six persons in one breath — γράφω-γράφεις-γράφει-γράφουμε-γράφετε-γράφουν.
