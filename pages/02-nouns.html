02 · Nouns (Ουσιαστικά) — the A2 declensions

⚠ New to cases? What each case actually does is on page 5 — read that first if Nominative / Accusative / Genitive / Vocative mean nothing to you yet.

Gender is not guessable from meaning — learn every noun with its article. Quick heuristic (about 85% reliable):

EndingUsuallyExample
-ος, -ας, -ης, -ες, -ουςmasculineο δρόμος, ο άντρας, ο μαθητής, ο καφές, ο παππούς
-α, -η, -ούfeminineη ώρα, η νίκη, η αλεπού
-ο, -ι, -μαneuterτο βιβλίο, το παιδί, το μάθημα

⚠ Vocative is left out on purpose — it is only for calling someone, and it is in the per-type tables below. ⚠ τον and την drop the -ν before some consonants (page 3); masculine τον is kept in writing because it is what separates τον δρόμο from το δρόμο.
