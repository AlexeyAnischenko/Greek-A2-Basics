05 · Adjectives (Επίθετα)

Rule: the adjective agrees with its noun in gender + case + number, and goes before the noun. ο καλός φίλος · τη μεγάλη πόλη · των ωραίων παιδιών

At A2 you need two patterns and two footnotes. Counted across the KLIK A2 wordlist:

B · -ος / -α / -ο (ωραίος)24learn it, it is only the feminine that differs

⚠ The accent in the cell is the one the row's word carries, not part of the type. καλός is end-stressed, so its endings show the accent; όμορφος is stem-stressed and keeps its stress throughout — όμορφος, όμορφη, όμορφου, όμορφων. Adjective stress never moves.

⚠ ζηλιάρης is the only type whose masculine plural is not in -οι. It takes the -ηδες of the masculine nouns in -ης (ο μανάβης → οι μανάβηδες), its feminine is exactly type B, and its neuter inserts -ικ- before the ending. No adjective of this type is in the KLIK wordlist or in the 2015–2018 exam papers — it is here to be recognised, not drilled.

⚠ γλυκός has only one row because only its feminine is different. Its masculine and neuter are plain type A: γλυκός / γλυκό / γλυκού / γλυκοί / γλυκούς / γλυκών. Same for ξανθός and Κρητικός.

⚠ The whole of A2 is the A and B rows — the first two of each gender. 191 of the 195 adjectives in the KLIK A2 wordlist are type A or type B; γλυκός, ξανθός and Κρητικός are 3 more, and ελαφρύς is the last one. Types C and D are in the grid so you can recognise them, not so you can drill them.

B · Type -ος / -α / -ο (ωραίος — nice)

The one you will actually meet instead: -ός / -ιά / -ό

γλυκός (sweet) → γλυκός / γλυκιά / γλυκό, plural γλυκοί / γλυκιές / γλυκά. Same feminine -ιά, but the masculine and neuter stay in type A. Also ξανθός (blond, feminine ξανθή or ξανθιά) and Κρητικός.

The one the books skip: -ης / -α / -ικο

ζηλιάρης (jealous) → ζηλιάρης / ζηλιάρα / ζηλιάρικο, plural ζηλιάρηδες / ζηλιάρες / ζηλιάρικα. The stress never moves off ζηλιά-.

These are the character adjectives — they describe what someone is like, so they turn up in speech far more than in a coursebook. The productive group is -ιάρης:

GreekEnglishGreekEnglish
ζηλιάρηςjealousγκρινιάρηςgrumpy, whiny
τεμπέληςlazyκλαψιάρηςcry-baby
πεισματάρηςstubbornπαραπονιάρηςalways complaining
φοβητσιάρηςeasily frightenedγλυκούληςsweet (affectionate)

⚠ The masculine looks like a noun and behaves like one. ζηλιάρης declines exactly like ο μανάβης; -ιάρης words are in fact used as nouns just as often (είναι ζηλιάρης = he is a jealous one). The neuter -ικο is the giveaway that it is an adjective: a noun in -ης has no neuter at all.
