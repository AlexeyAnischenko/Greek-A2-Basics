How to read it: pick the gender band, take the article and the noun ending from a NOUN row and the adjective ending from an ADJ row of the same colour — του καλού φίλου, τις καλές ώρες, των καλών παιδιών.   Only the noun rows carry an article — the adjective borrows the noun’s.   Hover any cell for a sentence using that exact form.   Click any cell to copy it as plain text — in the noun grid, click one article or the ending to take just that.   The two endings rarely match. του καλού φίλου happens to rhyme; της καλής ώρας does not. What has to agree is gender, case and number — never the letters.
