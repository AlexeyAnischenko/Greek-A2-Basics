A group: -ω verbs with the stress before the ending (ανοίγω, γράφω); the past simple takes the ψ / ξ / σ of the aorist grid, the past continuous keeps the present stem. B1: -άω verbs; B2: -ώ verbs - both take -ούσα in the past continuous. Irregular: the endings are the A ones, the stem changes - see the 20 core irregular verbs and the full irregular list. 3rd plural: -αν and the spoken -ανε are both correct. Click any cell to copy it.
